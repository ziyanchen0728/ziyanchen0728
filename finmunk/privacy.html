Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 27, 2026

Who we are and scope

FinMunk is an independently developed personal bookkeeping app operated by Ziyan Chen. This policy covers the app and this support website. Contact: ziaynchen189@gmail.com.

Your ledger and device storage

Accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, exchange rates, custom account images and preferences are stored on your device. Demo and real ledgers are kept separately. The current version does not provide automatic bank connections, live prices or developer-operated ledger cloud sync. iOS device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Optional Apple sign-in

You can use the local ledger without signing in. If you choose Sign in with Apple, the app requests your name and stores the Apple user identifier and supplied name locally for your profile. Apple handles authentication; FinMunk does not receive your Apple password. Signing out does not erase ledger data.

Optional AI and international processing

AI is disabled when the device region or App Store storefront is mainland China, and when storefront eligibility cannot be confirmed. In eligible regions, using the AI assistant or testing its connection sends requests directly to api.deepseek.com using your configured API key. Requests can include your message, up to 10 recent conversation turns, available account and book names and currencies, pending drafts, recent action results and calculated financial query results. The full transaction database, images and bank card numbers are not automatically attached, but anything you type may be transmitted. Review the applicable DeepSeek Open Platform terms before using AI; processing may involve other countries, including China. We do not promise provider-side zero retention or exclusion from model training. Financial changes require confirmation in the app. Conversations and drafts are saved locally and excluded from ledger backups. API keys are kept in the device Keychain and can be cleared in AI service settings.

Camera, images and speech

Camera and image selection are used only when you choose them. Balance images are recognized on device with Apple Vision and are not sent to DeepSeek. Saving receipt images requests permission to add to Photos. Speech features request microphone and speech recognition permissions when used. On-device transcription is preferred; Apple may process audio when unavailable locally. The app does not save recordings or send audio to DeepSeek. Transcribed text that you send is handled as an AI message. Permissions can be managed in iOS Settings.

Exports, sharing and support

JSON backups, CSV exports and receipt images are created when requested and saved or shared to destinations you choose. These files may contain financial information; manage them carefully. If you email support, the developer and email service process the address, message and attachments you choose to provide to respond and resolve the request. We keep support correspondence as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion.

Your choices, retention and deletion

You can edit or delete entries, export backups and stop using optional services. To remove app-local data, delete the app rather than offloading it; separately delete exported files and device backups you control. Clear an AI key in settings before uninstalling, because Keychain items may survive app removal. Revoking Apple sign-in does not itself delete the ledger. Contact us about support correspondence or privacy requests; data already sent to a provider is subject to that provider’s applicable processes.

Advertising, website and updates

The current app does not integrate advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not use data for cross-app advertising tracking. This static website has no analytics scripts or forms; GitHub Pages may process request logs such as IP addresses under GitHub’s privacy statement. The app is a general personal finance tool, not a service directed at children. We update this policy when relevant functionality or data practices change.
