Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 27, 2026

Who we are and scope

FinMunk is an independently developed personal bookkeeping app operated by Ziyan Chen. This policy covers the app and this support website. Contact: ziaynchen189@gmail.com.

Your ledger and device storage

Accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, exchange rates, custom account images and preferences are saved on your device. Demo and real ledgers are separate. Optional cloud sync is described below. Automatic bank connections and live prices are not provided. iOS device backups follow your Apple settings.

Optional iCloud sync

If cloud sync is configured and you enable it, your real ledger, including accounts, custom icons, transactions, bills, budgets, books, asset snapshots, savings plans, goals and wishes, is sent to the private CloudKit database of your current Apple Account. Demo data, AI conversations, API keys and Apple sign-in credentials are excluded. Apple processes this data under its iCloud terms. Turning sync off keeps local and cloud copies. Conflicting edits require your choice; recovery copies are retained locally and can be exported. No ledger sharing with other users is provided.

Optional Apple sign-in

You can use the local ledger without signing in. If you choose Sign in with Apple, the app requests your name and stores the Apple user identifier and supplied name locally for your profile. Apple handles authentication; FinMunk does not receive your Apple password. Signing out does not erase ledger data. Deleting your account in Settings → Account removes the Apple user identifier and nickname stored on this device. Local ledgers, iCloud copies and subscriptions are kept. Revoke Apple authorization in your Apple Account and manage subscriptions in the App Store separately.

Optional AI and international processing

AI is unavailable for mainland China device regions or App Store storefronts, or when eligibility is unknown. In eligible regions, overseas membership includes developer-provided DeepSeek access; no personal API key is needed. After your explicit agreement, messages, up to 10 recent conversation turns, relevant account and book names and currencies, pending drafts, action results and calculated query results pass through FinMunk’s Railway-hosted server to DeepSeek. Anything you type may be sent; full transaction databases, card numbers and images are not attached automatically. The server verifies signed purchase credentials with Apple. Redis stores hashed connection/subscription identifiers and temporary rate counters for about 60 seconds. Application code does not persist chat bodies or log prompts, credentials or financial data; hosting and provider infrastructure may process connection metadata. Processing can involve other countries, including China. DeepSeek’s terms govern provider retention and processing; we do not promise zero retention or exclusion from training. Financial changes require separate confirmation. Conversations remain locally stored and excluded from ledger backups. Withdraw permission in Settings → AI Service to stop new requests; previously sent data remains subject to provider policies.

Camera, images and speech

Camera and image selection are used only when you choose them. Balance images are recognized on device with Apple Vision and are not sent to DeepSeek. Saving receipt images requests permission to add to Photos. Speech features request microphone and speech recognition permissions when used. On-device transcription is preferred; Apple may process audio when unavailable locally. The app does not save recordings or send audio to DeepSeek. Transcribed text that you send is handled as an AI message. Permissions can be managed in iOS Settings.

Exports, sharing and support

JSON backups, CSV exports and receipt images are created when requested and saved or shared to destinations you choose. These files may contain financial information; manage them carefully. If you email support, the developer and email service process the address, message and attachments you choose to provide to respond and resolve the request. We keep support correspondence as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion.

Your choices, retention and deletion

You can edit or delete entries, export backups and stop using optional services. To remove app-local data, delete the app rather than offloading it; separately delete exported files and device backups you control. Revoking Apple sign-in does not itself delete the ledger. Contact us about support correspondence or privacy requests; data already sent to a provider is subject to that provider’s applicable processes.

Advertising, website and updates

The current app does not integrate advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not use data for cross-app advertising tracking. This static website has no analytics scripts or forms; GitHub Pages may process request logs such as IP addresses under GitHub’s privacy statement. The app is a general personal finance tool, not a service directed at children. We update this policy when relevant functionality or data practices change.
