Help & support

Questions about your ledger, backups or account balances? Contact the developer directly. No GitHub account is needed.

Email support

Do I need an account?

No. Choose “Continue with the local ledger” on the sign-in screen. Apple sign-in is optional.

How do I back up or restore?

Open Profile → Backup & restore. Export a full JSON backup and keep it somewhere safe. Restore into an empty ledger after reviewing the preview. CSV is a transaction export, not a full backup.

Why is an account balance different?

Only recorded transactions and manual balance reconciliations affect your ledger. Confirm opening balances, transfers, refunds and exchange rates. FinMunk does not connect to your bank.

Why is AI unavailable?

AI is disabled in mainland China and when regional eligibility is unknown. In other eligible regions, a working DeepSeek API key and network connection are required; provider charges may apply.

How do I remove my information?

Delete individual records in the app, or delete the app to remove its local container. Manage exported files and device backups separately, and clear any AI key in settings before uninstalling. Email us for privacy requests concerning support correspondence.
